About Our Masjid

Serving the community since 1995

Masjid Al-Noor is a welcoming Islamic centre located in Baghdad, Iraq. Dedicated to worship, education, and community service, we serve families and individuals seeking spiritual growth, knowledge, and meaningful connection.

Our programmes include daily Salah, Quran classes for all ages, Islamic school, youth groups, and regular community outreach. We strive to embody the values of mercy, justice, and compassion taught in the Quran and Sunnah.
